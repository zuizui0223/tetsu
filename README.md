# tetsu — Railway-mediated plant redistribution

**Research question:** Do railway networks connect distant plant communities through propagule transport, beyond railway-side habitat, roads, urbanization, ports and recorder bias?

**Status: 2026-11-08, research v1.5. Official GIS source-backed rail/road habitat sampling candidates and reproducible secondary historical microhabitat findings; NO confirmed railway-driven dispersal, management, flowering or adaptive biological effect.**

## Mechanisms to distinguish
- **Transport:** seeds on freight/rolling stock, cargo contaminants/spillage, people and maintenance equipment.
- **Habitat:** disturbed railway verges allow ruderal/alien species to establish without any train-carried seed.
- **Confounding:** stations co-occur with ports, roads, cities, trade, gardens and heavy botanical observation.

## Predicted signatures
1. **Rail habitat:** railway sites have elevated alien plant occurrence/richness compared with matched road and background habitats.
2. **Rail transport network (primary):** connected, active railway regions share more alien plant species than otherwise comparable unconnected regions, after controlling for road travel, geographic distance, urbanization, climate, and recording effort; contrast native plant similarity.
3. **Cargo/ports:** signatures should be stronger for freight/port-linked regions and accidentally introduced taxa than for passenger-only rail and ornamental pathways.
4. **Historical test (conditional):** newly connected regions gain species records after line openings compared with credible temporal controls; first record is NOT arrival.

An association does not prove that a seed travelled by train.

The direction is present separately in warm (**13.5×**) and cold (**8.9×**) railway-region subgroups. [Evidence provenance](docs/japan_rail_microhabitat_reanalysis_v10.md).
- **[New real modern citizen-photo source audit](docs/japan_rail_weed_photo_feasibility_v10.md):** Tokyo/Osaka/Sendai in fixed city rectangles, 9 rail-weed taxa/groups, 2018–2025 iNaturalist photographic occurrences. - **[New horsetail spore ROI](docs/horsetail_spore_roi_inputs_v10.md):** *Equisetum* does not make flowers or seeds. Angiosperm flowering-mask pipeline now rejects Equisetum flower labels; [separate spore-cone/sterile shoot ROI](scripts/extract_horsetail_spore_roi.py) analyzes human-verified PNG masks without ever relabeling cones as flowers. Its railway microhabitat association therefore sharpens the independent **establishment/management filtering** question, rather than confirming train-carried seeds. The original **angiosperm seed dispersal / freight / ports** analysis remains unresolved.

Even the strict near-rail pixel comparison has no independently replicated treatment or justifiable effect estimator.
- **Actual NDVI source QC, NOT a railway effect:** strict near20m n=1 shows observed relative 2017→2024/25 NDVI differences **−0.208449/−0.255576**, but near35m n=2 shows **+0.049537/+0.021190**; each source subset is just one correlated urban LRT segment. **Sampling offset changes direction**, so do not infer plant survival, mowing, seed transport, railway gust adaptation or phenology effects.
- **[Historic GSI source availability](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42445789140):** year-labelled air-photo GSI Z17 tiles **0/20 available** in each of 2016, 2017, 2018 and 2024, versus **20/20 real nonblank tiles from modern seamlessphoto** at the exact same XYZ tile coordinates. Year-specific imagery coverage, not coordinate transformation, is absent in this official tile service; mosaic images cannot date a 2017 physical road reliably.
- **Historical OpenStreetMap source restriction:** HeiGIT's legacy ohsome v1 geometry extraction returns 403 (provider restriction, not empty road records). The successor API requires a user API key. [Fail-closed real source run](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42446226849) stopped after the first provider 403 rather than inventing OSM zero roads; the six fixed 20m and 35m rail-offset sites were all within 25m of a real official 2024 N13 road (6/6 per offset). Therefore **2017 road network presence/absence is UNKNOWN**, not zero; archived OSM mapping omissions would not prove historical physical road absence even if extraction were available. The script [`audit_lrt_2017_osm_road_history.py`](scripts/audit_lrt_2017_osm_road_history.py) fails closed and reports unavailable historical data, while retaining independently measured 2024 N13 context.
- **Scientific selection:** This urban street-running LRT is poor for an independent *railway-verge versus road-verge vegetation* treatment. Prioritize a **non-road-adjacent railway corridor**, actual rail right-of-way and matched road-only plots, repeated fixed-view photos with documented management, and separate propagule transport histories. The previous Japanese horsetail trackbed-versus-embankment observation remains a stronger **ecological source pattern**, not refuted by this tram's source limitations. No biological causal effect has been established in v1.4.

## v1.5 — real non-road ordinary rail corridors in three Japan regions

[Full official N02 conventional railway versus N13 road-source audit](docs/japan_ordinary_rail_road_separation_v15.md) · [successful real source run](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42447780719)

- **Corrected the urban tram road co-location failure from v1.4.** Three pre-fixed geographically independent Japanese study regions (Sendai/Miyagi, Niigata, Nagano) were analyzed from actual MLIT N02 2025 **ordinary passenger railway** lines, N13 September 2024 road centrelines and official N02 station lines. Subway, public municipal urban railway (operator category 3), new high-speed rail, monorails and trams explicitly excluded. A first pilot incorrectly included Sendai Tozai subway because its route name lacked "地下鉄"; the code/test now exclude it by operator category.
- N13 real source roads: **183,706** locally indexed parts in Sendai, **192,241** in Niigata, and **232,428** in Nagano. Across **370** fixed 35m-near-rail/1600m-road-control source transect sides, **10** satisfied strict GIS-only road separation: near ≥75m from a modern road and ≥300m from a railway station, far ≤50m from an N13 road and ≥1000m from any passenger railway.
- **Positive, source-backed geographic eligibility:** **仙山線 2** (Miyagi), **磐越西線 4** (Niigata), **吾妻線 2**, **しなの鉄道線 1**, **篠ノ井線 1** (Nagano study rectangle). This is a shortlist of **five ordinary rail route groups across three separated geographies**. It is NOT evidence of five independent treatments, confirmed at-grade trackside vegetation, trains transporting seeds, cutting adaptation or biological changes.
- **[Real 2025 Sentinel-2 C1 SCL/NDVI source verification](docs/japan_ordinary_rail_s2_current_v15.md)** · [source Action #42448654924](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42448654924): all **10/10 preselected road-separated candidate near pixels** were classified as SCL=4 vegetation on ≥1 of four prechosen 2025 summer acquisitions; each also had a road-only far pixel classified SCL4 on the SAME date. 5/10 near pixels had SCL4 on ≥2 dates (Sendai 1/2, Niigata 0/4 due to cloud/source availability, Nagano 4/4). Across contemporaneous SAME-DATE pairs, near-minus-road NDVI medians were **+0.164881 Sendai (n2), −0.024184 Niigata (n4), +0.031872 Nagano (n4)**. Exactly **5/10 individual source pairs positive and 5/10 negative**: there is NO demonstrated uniform railway vegetation advantage. This is a successful real mixed-pixel vegetation-source selection gate, not railway grass/quadrat cover, biological dispersal, wind adaptation or railway treatment effect. Near 35m may still be forest canopy over tunnels, viaduct surroundings, garden or nonrail land. COG UTM53 vs official UTM54 site mismatch was caught and corrected with tests. Candidate points remain private to CI; no raw coordinates/images deposited.
- Main railway **seed and spore dispersal** hypothesis continues to require active rail/freight movement data and actual propagules/complete plant surveys; habitat filtering/mowing/wind/reproductive ROI remain independent threads, not confirmed by proximity to a mapped rail line.

## v1.6 — ESA 2021 land-cover correction: 9/10 strict 35m rail candidates are forest, 2 posthoc grassland leads

[Original fixed 10-source WorldCover crosscheck](docs/japan_ordinary_rail_worldcover_2021_v16.md) · [full 370-site exploratory source audit](docs/japan_worldcover_370_site_posthoc_discovery_v16.md) · [actual 2025 Sentinel/GSI check of two new sites](docs/japan_two_grass_sites_S2_GSI_v16.md)

- **Actual official ESA WorldCover 2021 v200 10m COG land-cover data** physically sampled at the identical 10 original, N02/N13 road-isolated, 2025 Sentinel-2 SCL4 green pixels across Sendai/Miyagi, Niigata and Nagano. **9/10 near-track 35m 2021 classes are tree cover; 1/10 is mapped permanent water; 0/10 mapped grassland or herbaceous wetland**, despite all ten having been classed 2025 SCL4 vegetation. The 3x3 WorldCover pixel surroundings have corresponding tree/plurality classifications. This reveals the **tree-canopy/land-cover confound**, not confirmed absence of any ground-layer herbaceous plant or a 2021→2025 true water→grass change. [Source #42453959307](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42453959307).
- **Posthoc exploratory source audit** of the **originally sampled 370 geographic rail/road normals** (NOT independent random railway experiments): 2021 near35m classes include **147 tree, 24 mapped grassland, 117 built-up, 73 cropland, 8 permanent water and 1 bare/sparse**. The 2021 tree-cover fraction among eligible samples rose **147/370=39.7%** in all source normals → **14/19=73.7%** when requiring an eligible 2024 road-only control and near-road separation ≥50m → **9/10=90.0%** at original ≥75m. Stringent road isolation strongly changes **forest landscape composition**, a serious sampling confound, not railway-caused forest cover. [Actual source #42454338343](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42454338343).
- Relaxing only the 2024 road separation threshold from original **75m to 50m**, while retaining station/rail and road-only control constraints, yields **2 posthoc near35m 2021 WorldCover-grassland source candidates**, one on **信越線 (Niigata)** and one on **小海線 (Nagano)**. These were selected after inspecting a land-cover result and therefore are **site-reconnaissance leads, NOT confirmatory rail-effect observations**.
- **[Actual two-candidate source cross-check #42454635103](https://github.com/zuizui0223/tetsu/actions/runs/42454635103):** 信越線 near and road-control both had 2025 SCL4 vegetation on **2025-07-30**; near NDVI **0.378209** vs far road NDVI **0.875961** (single descriptive near−far **−0.497752**, NO railway treatment effect). 小海線 near did **not** meet 2025 SCL4 on the four selected summer acquisitions; status is not proof of grass loss or mowing. **Official GSI current seamless aerial JPEG imagery is actually available at Z17/Z18 for both locations** (Z19 unavailable); no high-res vegetation/track grade was visually verified, and no original image bytes or site coordinates are republished.
- **Scientific next gate:** operator-approved or independently licensed **at-grade railway verge versus overhanging forest canopy / bridge / field / river** ground-truth review using time-stamped aerial/current field evidence, real fixed-plot taxon/cutting/herbicide labels and road-verge controls. The main rail **freight/ports propagule transport** and separate mowing/wind/photo-reproductive plasticity hypotheses remain unconfirmed; coarse GIS 35m proximity or remote grassland class alone cannot establish them.

# v351: 陸地等面積格子×全球気候帯の層化確率抽出（2026-11-24、元標本前に固定）

## Source of bias being addressed
v348–350 investigated 6 *convenience metro anchor windows*, not a global target sample; although actual 2025 iNat×Overture got 1,041 original public plant detections and both spatial arms supported only Prague, those are NOT a globally representative sample. The previously reanalyzed 7 independent railway flora papers were similarly source-availability selected.

## New sampling frame **BEFORE** iNaturalist or observed railway biology
- Original land/country geometry: **Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 countries**, open [nvkelso/natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector), pinned original revision `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`, file `geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson` at that revision. Continent field `CONTINENT`; combine each of the six inhabited continents, exclude Antarctica and low-level source labels with missing continent. Areas where coarse 1:110m source does not represent tiny islands/coasts are necessarily outside this first pilot's population.
- WorldClim 2.1 1970–2000 long-term baseline, 10 arc-minute BIO1 annual mean temperature from `https://geodata.ucdavis.edu/climate/worldclim/2_1/base/wc2.1_10m_bio.zip` SHA256 **`00513224583665ec0f2f955a4ec252730c4deb2004cce9e793492a3f26df4dcf`**, previously original-run authenticated by v342; source ZIP must hash-match again. For original raster units, validate against original GEO tags and frozen two geographically distant realistic control points; **do not treat 10x scaling as degrees Celsius**. Climate bands fixed in °C at **cool <8; temperate 8≤T<20; warm ≥20**.
- Equal-area source frame: **EPSG:6933** global cylindrical equal-area CRS, 100km×100km land-centre grid from fixed x/y bounds `[-17,000,000,17,000,000]` metres and `[-7,300,000,7,300,000]` metres. Cell centers must be inside a Natural Earth country polygon of one single continent and have valid WorldClim BIO1. **All** remaining candidate cells, including cells with no mapped railway and no public plants, belong to a fully enumerated sampling population. No original rail or plant occurrence data are consulted in frame generation. 100 km cell-centre sampling deliberately excludes partial coastline and isolated cells; it estimates opportunity for this defined centre-on-land cell frame, **not all global land or railway length**.
- Fix `seed` string `tetsu-v351-20261124-global-landcell-temperature-before-rail-plant-observations`, and draw **two distinct cells without replacement per continent×climate band** from the full finite numbered frame; if a climate stratum contains fewer than two cells, keep the actual count and explicitly report low support, with inclusion probability selected_n / eligible_population_n. A frame is a 6×3=18 climate/continent cross-classification; at most **36 cells**, with equal chance within each finite stratum. The selected cell coordinates (rounded to 0.1° only) and stable source cell-ID SHA are published because these are **100-km grid *sampling candidates* unrelated to plant/person locations**, not precise endangered species observation positions.
- Original source checks: official country GeoJSON provenance and SHA, the full 19-TIFF WorldClim archive SHA, actual BIO1 raster sample min/max, six continental source polygon support, all finite stratum sizes, number drawn, no duplicates, reproducible RNG, not chosen by railway presence or species. A missing source/bad raster/no cells must FAIL, never emit invented temperature or zero biological effect.

## What this does NOT yet do
- **Not network-length-proportional railway sampling.** No worldwide census of all original Overture rail segments or global denominator of track kilometres is yet enumerated. The new design is random *land grid cell* sampling. A later predeclared rail check against Overture 2026-11-18 transportation subtype=rail will classify each selected source cell as rail-present/absent. Rail-absent cells stay in the denominator. If track present, randomly select original rail segments **conditional on the sampled cell**, using clipped geometry length as weights. Without the denominator of rail length in all cells, do not extrapolate to global railway network kilometres or all train-operated railway flora.
- **Not a plant thermal ecological effect.** The source original WorldClim BIO1 characterizes macroclimate of the cell, NOT plant species thermal niche. Without independent global species T (Harris EIDC original CSV still incomplete due server interrupted ZIP) plus source-complete local plant/rail/nonrail botanical surveys, no statement about warmth, train seed movement or biological adaptations.
- **No urban/rural control yet.** Sampling across all land-cell centers reduces the previous metropolitan-selection filter, but will yield many rural and rail-free candidate cells and is not itself stratified by land cover or observer effort. Future source GHSL/population must be added as a distinct pre-outcome design phase before estimating effects.
- **No self-extracted vegetation plots.** An iNaturalist record is a presence with observer effort bias, not a full site-wide community inventory with absence, plot area or biomass. 2025 plants vs 2026 mapped rail is a temporal mismatch. Source geoprivacy/obscured and rail-access rules remain mandatory.

## Complete outcomes and go/no-go
**v351 source frame success** = verified original land + WorldClim archives, enumerated complete 100 km centre-land global population with 18 expected strata, selected <=36 cells reproducibly, audit original map and climate units. **v352 rail source success** (separate downstream) = Overture queried for each original selected cell without choosing replacements based on rails/plants; no rail cells reported as rail-free only when network source is genuinely complete; source failures are missing data. Original public plant API follow-up comes after rail sample, never the reverse.

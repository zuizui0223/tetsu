## What is original?

The full-source railway frame, same-scale independent spatial comparisons, cross-taxon observation-process control, source-level observer-disjoint split, and annual matched comparisons are constructed for this study. The analyses are not just reanalysis of one published railway flora. Original data synthesis does not make these observational associations causal. Source flora studies remain external context, not pooled independent confirmation.

## Bird manuscript gate

Working question: Is the association between mapped railway connections and avian community similarity stronger among birds with low hand-wing index? Avoid "dispersal capacity mediates" because neither movement nor mediation is measured. Provisional descriptive title: "Wing morphology and avian community similarity across global railway-connected landscapes". HWI is a morphological proxy; not observed flight ability or dispersal distance.

Current conditional test run 45584474048: in progress at this commit, no conditional coefficient available. It reconstructs the frozen 537/424 HWI support, compares high versus low HWI within jointly supported habitat-density x migration strata, using fixed per-pair weights across both groups and all years. Resident-only and dense-resident sensitivities. Preserve the original support threshold (20 residual crossing blocks). Do not lower it to recover significance.

Decision rules after results:
- If joint-stratum support is insufficient: report not identifiable, do not interpret as effect absent; no claim independent of habitat and migration. Stop reslicing the same years.
- If direct conditional HWI contrast is uncertain or reverses: keep the unconditioned association as exploratory; do not claim a mobility-related mechanism.
- If it remains negative (high minus low), with coherent direct status contrast and land/metric sensitivities: report conditional association, not movement, and only then consider a distinct taxonomic/range-size validation. No independent confirmation because all years informed discovery.
- To strengthen inference, seek a predeclared source or heldback temporal/geographic evaluation. Do not call the same observation platform, same geography, same years, or same-derived traits independent replication. No eBird.
- Fine-scale local habitat and railway operating data would be required before mechanism claims. No post-hoc threshold tuning, no additional raw scan solely to rescue a null.

Plant and bird findings may be organized as different responses to infrastructure and observation access, but the plant–bird difference is not established in heldback years. Do not combine the two into a claimed taxon-level causal contrast. The plant paper should not depend on bird traits as a confirmed mechanism.

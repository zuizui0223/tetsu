**To:** jana.majekova@savba.sk

**Subject:** Research data / collaboration inquiry: 2025 Flora paper on Slovak railway flora and freight traffic

Dear Dr. Májeková,

I am conducting a comparative reanalysis of vascular-plant communities along railway networks, focusing on whether rail traffic relates to the sharing of *the same alien plant species* between stations, after accounting for geography and the regional species pool.

I read your 2025 *Flora* article, “Climate and traffic volume control plant species richness in railway areas” (doi:10.1016/j.flora.2025.152780), with great interest. Your finding that alien plant richness relates to cargo-train traffic provides an especially valuable basis for an independent test of between-station compositional similarity.

Would it be possible to obtain, for the 30 surveyed stations, (1) a station-by-species presence/absence table including native, archaeophyte and neophyte classifications; (2) the station-level freight and passenger train counts used in the analysis, including units, observation years, and whether the figures refer to movements through or stops at stations; and (3) the station identifiers, sampling years, station area/locations, and available environmental/management covariates? The data need not be publicly redistributed; a restricted-use research dataset with appropriate attribution would be entirely suitable. I would also appreciate knowing whether the sites overlap the 2012–2014 Poprad–Plaveč 19-station survey reported in Májeková & Limánek (2016).

Our intended analyses would retain stations—not the many dependent pairs of stations—as the independent traffic-exposure units, evaluate alien and native species separately, and compare observed station-to-station similarity with expectations conditioned on station richness and species prevalence. No causal train-mediated seed dispersal would be inferred from correlations alone. I would be happy to share an analysis plan and discuss collaborative use of the data in accordance with any conditions you specify.

Thank you for considering this request.

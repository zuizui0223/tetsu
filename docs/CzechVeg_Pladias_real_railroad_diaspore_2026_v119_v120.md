# tetsu v11.9–v12.0: authentic CzechVeg + Pladias plant trait join, independent railway-text versus road-text habitat diagnostic

## Bottom line: genuine independent same-strategy source, but NOT a confirmed same-design biological replication

**Yes, the public original Czech vegetation × same Sádlo (2018) dispersal strategy taxon-level join WORKED.** We verified the unmodified official 2026 complete CzechVeg plot and species source checksums, downloaded the live Pladias plant dispersal and life-form XLSX, required matching original source semantics across the generated spreadsheets, and linked original source accepted names **without fuzzy, genus-only or missing-trait imputation**. We could calculate descriptive true plant species preference differences across **RAIL-associated versus ROAD-associated plot text labels** at two levels of textual precision, separately across source phytogeographic regions.

**No, this does NOT reproduce Slovenia's fixed 89 paired within-railway ballast shoulder (BS) versus ballast toe (BT) source design.** CzechVeg plots are a heterogeneous, independently assembled national set of vegetation relevés with different plot areas, survey dates, terrestrial habitats, likely some off-right-of-way railway associations, and different source sampling efforts; the land-use/management/physical-rail attribution is not externally validated. The observable railway-vs-road dispersal guild contrasts are small, heterogeneous and **do not show a geographically robust weighting-based sign reversal**. The result is evidence of **source feasibility and non-generalizability across this different design**, not an ecological falsification of the Slovenian within-track observation.

### Independent descriptive outcome: Czech RAIL-associated TEXT vs ROAD-associated TEXT prevalence, not track-vs-toe

For every original Czech source species with both unambiguous original Pladias strategy and lifeform, compute difference between fraction of eligible **rail-labelled plots** and eligible **road-labelled plots** recording the species; compare Sádlo strict wind group Epilobium+Phragmites against other dispersal strategies; adjust descriptively by original **therophyte** vs other lifeforms (proxy for annual, **NOT** Slovenian strict annual classification). Two defensible species weighting definitions: equal species versus source plot occupancy frequency. No source site was randomly assigned to railway vs road, or matched by date/habitat/area/rail maintenance.

| Original Czech source text strategy | Eligible RAIL / ROAD plots | Known both-trait source taxa | Wind−other RAIL relative-prevalence contrast, **species-equal** | Same source taxa **occurrence weighted** | Weighting sign reversal? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| High-precision HABITAT rail vs road | **195 / 681** | **754** | **−0.0003306** | **−0.0174662** | **No** |
| Broader HABITAT or LOCALITY rail vs road | **1,580 / 5,672** | **1,437** | **+0.0011306** | **+0.0030148** | **No** |

These small source values are **not numerically comparable** to Slovenian same-site BS−BT scores (different source habitat, plot area, denominator, stratification). There is **no confirmed independent reproduction** of the original Slovenian wind-diaspore frequency-sign reversal from these Czech rail-vs-road candidates.

### v12.0 REAL source region balance, source-plot strata and no false robust replication

**Only those Czech source phyto-regions with >=5 original candidate RAIL and >=5 original candidate ROAD plots AND both wind/other lifespan classes supported** were eligible, then additionally compare groups of >=20 rail and >=20 road candidate plots; 8,000 source-region resamples and leave-one-original-region-out. Source regions are not independent random countries.

| Source source-region-balanced comparison | Eligible original source phyto-regions | Region-equal mean species-equal | Region-equal mean frequency-weighted | 95% region bootstrap interval for weight-estimand gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| HABITAT-only source high precision | **4** | **−0.0100385** | **−0.0351507** | **[+0.008475,+0.037357]** |
| HABITAT+LOCALITY broader source | **28** | **+0.0035166** | **−0.0003730** | **[−0.009869,+0.016837]** |
| Broader source, each eligible region >=20 rail and >=20 road candidates | **17** | **+0.0005911** | **−0.0080828** | **[−0.006376,+0.024258]** |

The broad source 28-region **region-equal** point estimates have signs opposite with equal species vs occurrence weights but their gap interval **includes zero**, and only **15/28** original leave-one-region-out recalculations preserve the signs. Importantly the v11.9 **pooled-country broad source** contrast is positive under BOTH weights. These are **different estimands**; selection of source region weight alone can alter aggregate sign. Do NOT promote a Czech "independent replicated weighting reversal"; this is precisely the sampling-unit artifact caution motivating tetsu.

In the source **4** original phyto-regions identifiable with both strict and broad source labels, the equal-species source sign agrees in only **2/4**, and occurrence-weighted sign also in **2/4**. Original broadening from habitat to site-locality source text changes actual target sampling population substantially; source rail and road label sensitivity prevents a robust causal statement. The high precision 4-region occurrence contrast 95% source-region interval **[−0.057230,−0.004642]** is negative within that tiny selected set, NOT geographic macro replication. Only original region 16 yielded positive strict wind preference contrast while 18a,20b,68 were negative; source labels and geology may explain differences without requiring a freight vector hypothesis.

### Next meaningful identification and reproducibility gate

1. **Within-CzechVeg**: match original RAIL and ROAD text candidate plots to compatible original plots on *area*, *survey year/period*, phytogeographic source region, vegetation formation, local climate/urbanization, and taxon coverage. Compare within-source grouped source regional estimates (not all pooled sites). Do not infer source plot geographic distance without original coordinates and validated site ownership.
2. **Railway-specific filter**: subdivide only genuinely validated railway corridor or track/embankment source microhabitats, ideally similar to Slovenian BS−BT original source. Broader RAIL versus ROAD study does **not** identify intra-railway wind sorting. Source record text should be inspected (sampled candidate rail plot descriptions/manual source orthophotos with permissions) and unconfirmed rail track areas left unknown.
3. **True dispersal/mowing mechanism**: obtain operational traffic/cargo along matched source active track sections and dates, live seed traps or physically collected vehicle-borne diaspores, or recorded management mowing dates, not just taxon functional guild proxies. Field source parent work and trait data provider rights respected; substantial Pladias analysis/manuscript needs Governing Board discussion.
4. **Original Slovenia vs Czech country analogy**: use same Sádlo dispersal archetypes but recognize different original sampling, and predeclare target biological estimator; test countries only after harmonizing plot zone and source measurement grain.

**Manuscript position:** Single-country source ecology in Slovenia is strong for source functional composition and a frequency-sensitive estimand, but Czech independent cross-study plot-type comparison is **near zero, source-label sensitive and geographically heterogeneous**.

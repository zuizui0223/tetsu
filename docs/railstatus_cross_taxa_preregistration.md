# Cross-taxon rail-status specificity (registered before results)

Primary comparison: plants versus birds, insects and fungi separately, on exact common 2025 100 km and 200 km eligible grid cells (same 50 observations / 5 observers / 20 species gate, same land and source coverage). Use 50% land as primary and 80% as sensitivity; repeat on all-four common support and each taxon's native support. All data come from the v369 frozen photo-linked research-grade source. The replay must reproduce all v369 plant observation, observer and species counts exactly and both source SHA-256 hashes.

Primary metric: conditional active-normal rail addition after inactive rail, subway, tram/light rail, roads, built-up area, forest, climate, latitude and own-taxon effort; compare relative continent-heldout MSE gains and signed within-continent log-richness slopes. The active-only addition to the road baseline and all other statuses are reported as well. Paired 1000 km block-cluster approximate intervals for the difference in signed active slopes; 39 shared band-shuffles for conditional active/inactive in pairwise common panels. Less than four eligible heldout continents is insufficient for global heldout interpretation.

A plant-specific positive effect is consistent with botanical mechanisms, but taxon-specific observer behavior and detectability remain alternative explanations. A shared positive effect is consistent with access/observation opportunity or shared ecological processes; it does not prove observer bias. Subway is an imperfect urban/access contrast; abandoned tagging and historical/current operation are imperfect. No direct inference on train dispersal, trackside communities, or species absences.

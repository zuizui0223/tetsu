## GBIF georeferenced data availability for taxon groups (not an ecological effect)

Deterministic evenly spaced 12 canonical-name selection per group (not random population sampling); each was queried via GBIF taxon match and per-taxon count:
- 139 accidental-only taxa: **11/12** high-confidence exact taxonomy matches; **4/12** have at least 20 Japanese georeferenced 2018–2025 occurrences.
- 351 ornamental-no-accidental taxa: **12/12** exact matches; **3/12** have at least 20 qualifying occurrences.

This exposes a serious sparse-occurrence problem for species-by-cell assemblages. **GBIF absence must never be coded as biological absence.** A full inferential test requires a DOI-bearing GBIF download or independently complete regional floristic checklists, survey effort diagnostics, species matching and likely pooling over more years/species.

## Freight network opportunity and a useful negative control

The Japanese official N02 network describes passenger railway geometry. JR Freight's official handling-station listing separately identifies 19 ORS locations, which operate as off-rail services. **ORS provide unusually informative road-supported logistics controls**: they are economically tied to the same freight operator but not necessarily railway-connected locally. Match ORS and directly rail-served sites within urbanization, port access and observer-effort strata rather than simply comparing any railway-adjacent site to wilderness.

JR Freight's 2026 corporate profile independently reports 73 lines, 7,666.7 operational km and 237 handling stations (2026-04-01), and 394 trains/day for the 2025-03 timetable; these are company-wide figures, NOT individual route traffic. Source: https://www.jrfreight.co.jp/about. Freight handling-service table: https://www.jrfreight.co.jp/service/area.

## Model hypothesis that the available data could test

`alien species cell presence / targeted observation intensity ~ rail-served freight station + ORS + passenger rail + road accessibility + port distance + urban area + climate + survey effort + spatial effect`

The key pre-specified interaction is a difference between **documented unintentional** and **ornamental escape** pathway groups at otherwise matched freight vs ORS vs passenger railway places. Without a full distribution and survey denominator, such a model would be unjustified.

## Outstanding blockers

1. Spatial geocoding of freight service stations and independent verification of *direct* freight train service. DO NOT geocode a station name to a passenger station of the same name without auditing.
2. Obtain matching road access, ports, land use, climate, and sampling intensity; ORS may be in different geography and is not automatically a matched control.
3. Reconcile species-name synonyms / varieties, establishment status, geospatial accuracy and records from herbarium vs observation sources.
4. A GBIF DOI-bearing download (or independent floras) and spatial blocking/endpoint resampling before testing differences.
5. Direct dispersal still cannot be inferred even after ecological correlations; a field sampling/genetics experiment would be the independent mechanism test.

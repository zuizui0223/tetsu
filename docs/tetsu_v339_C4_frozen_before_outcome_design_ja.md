## A priori taxon groups (EXACT normalized binomials, no genus-wide inference)
Strict candidate C4: `amaranthus caudatus; bothriochloa ischaemum; cynodon dactylon; cyperus rotundus; echinochloa colona; euphorbia prostrata; fimbristylis dichotoma; saccharum spontaneum; setaria viridis; sorghum halepense`.
Ambiguous candidate only in sensitivity: `euphorbia hispida`; requires species-level physiology/name confirmation; all other unlisted taxa are UNKNOWN, **never automatically C3**.
C3 Poaceae (small comparator panel, separately interpreted): `arundo donax; avena sativa; bromus japonicus; phleum pratense; poa annua; poa bulbosa`. No claim that other dicots are C3.
Source literature: Osborne et al. 2009 ecological selection C4 `doi:10.1098/rspb.2009.0307`, 2014 C3/C4 grass global reassessment `doi:10.1111/nph.12942`, Euphorbia C3/C4/C2 variation `doi:10.1093/jxb/err059`; species-level decisions require eventual dedicated verified trait ledger. This is an **exploratory first-screen** with candidate taxon labels; conclusions conditional on trait accuracy.

## Frozen analyses, no model search
1. For 2014 original source sites, compare Track and far Interior Plot 2 **paired fraction of strict C4 taxon occurrences among all source named taxa**, and paired fraction of C4 source-abundance number relative to same plot summed source abundance. Repeat 2017 as sensitivity; station and between strata explicitly separated. Such fractions are *detected C4 fractions*, not true overall C4 proportions, because unnamed/unknown taxa may also be C4.
2. For source-eligible 2014 EIVE >=3 systems, >=5 known/50% cover Track/Interior2 (v327 strict 20 site cohort), preserve same original eligible site IDs and compute full and C4-deleted equal-taxon EIVE mean (and source-abundance weighted mean). Allow EIVE CWM after deletion only for plots retaining >=1 qualifying non-C4 species; **do not change original site eligibility after seeing C4**; report exclusions after removal.
3. Repeat relaxed 2014 EIVE >=1 system, >=3 taxa/30% (31 sites) and sparse 2017 as sensitivities. 2014 station–between matched pairs identical to original v332/v334 G1 relaxed (14) and strict (5), plus G3 (7), 2017 sparse. Compare same 4-plot contrast with and without strict C4; report denominators and excluded pairs, never substitute new matching.
4. Leave-one-strict-C4-taxon-out checks, grasses-only C4 removal, candidate ambiguous Euphorbia addition. Report observed contribution but neither assume railway *causes* C4 nor that a C4 species is locally warm-adapted. EIVE was originally European-calibrated.
5. Null/stop gate: a **descriptive** >50% attenuation of the positive 2014 Track−Interior thermal centre plus positive paired strict-C4-fraction difference in both strict and relaxed 2014 supports further tests, conditional on robust taxon influence and adequate n. If C4 deletion preserves >=75% of both main occurrence-weight thermal contrasts or C4 fractions are not higher, **do not pursue C4 as main causal explanation**. The result must not be called a physiological mechanism without ballast temperatures and matched nonrail controls. Zero C4 coverage is a negative data-availability result, not support for C3 selection.

## Inferential limitations
One non-randomly sampled railway corridor; site dependence; EIVE Euro-centric in Kashmir; absence of functional pathway measurement, seed carriage, railway microclimate, C4 in situ physiology, population persistence or habitat randomization; 2014/2017 repeated same sites not independent studies. Primary objective is candidate pathway attribution only. This protocol was fixed after v339 verified 122 names but **before quantitative C4 site/abundance contrasts**.

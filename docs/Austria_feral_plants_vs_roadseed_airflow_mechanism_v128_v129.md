Biology limits:
- This source actually tracked **seed movement**, unlike Austrian 2016 plant presences, but **on a ROAD by CAR**, not by a passing train. The two wind morphology labels of the 2013 original species are not the same taxonomic classification as Pladias/Sádlo original species archetypes used in Slovenia and CzechVeg, so do not numerically pool trait groups as if equivalent.

- Original source 45m measurement maximum means physical longer tail may be right censored. *Ambrosia* seeds were intentionally heated to prevent germination; physical movement is NOT seed viability or realized plant establishment.

## Inference firewall and rigorous next goal

The observational process may be conceptualized as:

\[
P(\text{plant detected at railway plot})
 =
P(\text{viable seed arrives})
 \times P(\text{establishes}\mid\text{viable seed arrives})
 \times P(\text{observed}\mid\text{established})
\]

This is **a conceptual conditional factorization**, assuming no additional omitted transitions; not an estimated probability in any published field dataset here. It illustrates why physical dispersal (2007 direct ROAD seed traps and 2013 actual ROAD car seed movement), observed feral-plant sampling near Austrian railway stations (2016, **no measured input seed rain**), and railway vegetation surveys (original Slovenia 2026/CzechVeg 2026) cannot simply be multiplied or pooled into a **train dispersal → establishment → adaptation** causal mechanism. Their species, observation sites, exposure history, survey methods, responses and spatial sampling units are not matched.

Two authentic future source gates make the research an actual *railway process* macroecology paper:
1. **True viable diaspores transported by TRAIN**: actual original seed traps on train wagons, ballast/vehicle loading points, matched unexposed platform controls with viable germination, timestamped traffic/cargo route and meteorology. Source-derived motorway roadside seed experiments demonstrate biological plausibility but no railway-vehicle effect size.
2. **Same-site original railway field establishment filtering**: matched fixed-area BS/BT vs graded track/embankment true botanical plots in more than one country (France 2021 RER 394 original 2m² relevés track 128 vs embankment 266 in Rakotoson et al. 2025 [10.1111/wre.70025](https://doi.org/10.1111/wre.70025), original raw on request), plant life-history/dispersal trait source and true repeated mowing/gust exposure. Original surveys need author attribution and Pladias extensive use requires contact its original Governing Board.

# tetsu v11.3–v11.4: frequency-sensitive RAIL dispersal guild vs frequency-robust global ROAD alien enrichment

## Executive research decision

The clearest observational **Slovenian railway** result is a **guild-by-occupancy-frequency estimand reversal** among exact original source plants. A matching analysis of a separate original field-surveyed **global mountain ROAD** benchmark (different biological guild trait) produces a directionally consistent roadside alien-vs-native preference irrespective of species frequency weighting. **Do not call this a rail-specific wind mechanism or an identified direct rail-vs-road effect:** wind/other, alien/native, plot zone geometry and sampling area are different between sources. Both are descriptive reanalyses of OTHER original field researchers' published data.

## RAIL v11.3: original 261 source-taxa sign inversion remains under section / genus reweighting but has important uncertainty

Recreate **same original 261 source taxa** on 89 source railway field sites. Define one plant species' BS/BT preference as `(BS-only location count−BT-only location count)/(source location count observed BS or BT)`; original track left and right sides unioned within the field location first. Compare **strict wind−other** within **strict annual vs other known lifespan** strata under different weights:

- **All 261 exact SAME original taxa**: source species-equal contrast **+0.189388**, source occupancy-opportunity weighted **−0.098125** (readers must not conflate these different estimands or treat source site occupancy as biomass/individual count).
- **15 original RAIL sections positive-weight Bayesian bootstrap (2,500 draws)**: preserve ALL 261 recorded species with nonzero mass in every resample, so rare species are not spuriously discarded. Species-equal source interval **[+0.120812,+0.258620]** (always positive across the central 95%); source occupancy-weight interval **[−0.204419,+0.014174]** **includes zero**. Opposite signs together in **2,395/2,500 = 95.80%**. This is conditional on the original one-country non-random source and not globally generalizable uncertainty.
- **192 actual original source scientific genera independently positive-weight Bayesian reweighting (2,500 draws)**: genus-level source-sensitive species-equal interval **[+0.027384,+0.355253]**; occupancy opportunity interval **[−0.212231,+0.051204]** **includes zero**; source signs opposite in **2,239/2,500 = 89.56%**. Every one of **192/192** leave-one-genus-out source contrasts retains opposite directions. This genus proxy captures some taxonomy grouping but is NOT a phylogenetic covariance matrix or phylogenetic independent contrasts.

**Scope:** The species-equal positive sign is more stable than the occupancy-weight negative sign in these source-resampling diagnostics. The sign inversion is an **observed estimand property** and a source sensitivity, **not** a statistically settled universal wind-selection mechanism. Source original BS vs BT sampled widths have not been demonstrated equal; local observation/detection and taxa source frequency can affect results. No individual trait plasticity, fitness or wind-cargo seeds measured.

## ROAD v11.4: independent global original field benchmark does NOT reverse guild association simply from changing species weighting

Original MIREN v3 source uses matched **100m²** mountain-road Plot1 (road edge) and Plot3 (52–102m interior), not a railway track study. In each region species may have different Native/Alien status; therefore a plant's statistical unit is **region×source taxon**, not a fabricated globally fixed nativeness. For each such taxon define a paired observed plot preference `(roadside-only sites−interior-only sites)/sites present either plot`. Compare the mean **Alien−Native** within each original source region, under equal taxon weight vs frequency weight.

| Authentic original MIREN ROAD cohort | Independent Road+Region+Site units | Region×source-taxon status units | Eligible regions with ≥10 true matched sites and ≥3 Alien/Native taxa | Equal-species region-equal Alien−Native preference | Occupancy-opportunity region-equal Alien−Native preference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original first matched year per site | **1,005** | **6,917** | **21** | **+0.565648** | **+0.539690** |
| First eligible 2017-or-later visit per site (source-protocol quality sensitivity) | **845** | **5,847** | **18** | **+0.562345** | **+0.540581** |

**Every qualified source region:** 21/21 initial and 18/18 post-2017 has an **Alien−Native roadside preference positive under BOTH weights**. No source region sign reversal in those cohorts. Five-thousand original-region bootstrap draws yield:

This is a strong observational **within-original-field-survey ROADside Alien enrichment pattern**, supported by regional replication. It **rejects the simplistic idea that ANY strong positive transport-corridor guild sorting necessarily flips signs under species-frequency weighting**. It **does not establish** that railway-specific wind-trait effects are categorically unique (different data type and ecological guild). The post-2017 group is a **sensitivity window**, not wholly independent new sites because it overlaps the earlier first-year cohort. Not every original region qualifies, and 25 mountain regions/26 source codes must not be treated as 26 independent random countries.

## What this supports and the next strict identification gate

1. **Transport-corridor habitat sorting has different estimand sensitivity depending on outcome/trait, species incidence, and source study design.** TRUE road field-record alien/native contrast remains positive, while source RAIL wind/other trait contrast is frequency-dependent.
2. **RAIL source v10.6 site-level strict wind fraction decrease remains valid in its own estimand**, but the same plants are not uniformly more outer-zone preferring if all species are weighted equally. The original plants' site-frequency distribution matters.
3. **Mechanism is still unidentified**: source MIREN plots are mountain ROADS; Slovenian BS/BT are unequal/unspecified zone area. Neither source demonstrates train-borne viable propagules, actual train slipstream, cutting exposure, biomass recovery, flower phenology, heritable evolution or causal railway selection.
4. For an ecological cross-country **RAIL macro replication**, obtain a genuinely accessible independent original RAIL vegetation matrix, harmonize source plot area and site/zone distances and taxon life history, and evaluate the **same** wind/other trait and rarity interaction with rail section/country and phylogenetic/effort controls. The Kashmir Himalaya Dryad publisher metadata was verified by v11.1 but original raw download returned **401**, so no raw independent RAIL replication has been claimed. French RER original detailed matrix is available on author request only.
5. MIREN protocol source earlier than 2012 may not survey all natives completely; 2017+ results reduce but do not erase data missingness concerns. Scientific manuscript using MIREN data should notify original consortium steering and discuss original contributor involvement as requested in the publication.

**Proposed accurate manuscript framing:** *Species-frequency weighting reveals different functional-estimand sensitivities in railway plant assemblages and globally standardized roadside floras*. This is currently **a robust descriptive method-and-ecology comparison, not a paper proving that trains transport seeds, mowing selects annuals, or rail tracks induce wind adaptations**. Keep tetsu PR #1 DRAFT and original main unchanged.

# v346: Global (>200,000 plant species) thermal envelopes × true original railway floras — frozen design (2026-11-24)

## User question
Can railway plant "warm affinity" be analysed on a **worldwide climatic definition**, rather than applying European-only EIVE values to South Asia or Western Australia? **YES for a shared global species climate trait; NO yet for a causal, worldwide rail-vs-adjacent-natural vegetation selection effect.**

## Pre-effect inputs locked
**Species thermal trait:** Harris, Brummitt, Cobbold & Reeve (2022), *Bioclimatic envelopes for global plant species*, NERC Environmental Information Data Centre, DOI https://doi.org/10.5285/ca339c86-3674-4030-b891-35326e71141e. The original authors used 100+ million GBIF occurrences across >200k terrestrial plant species, climate at occurrence time and area, with published effort and MODIS EVI-based sampling-bias correction. The primary temperature trait will be a documented species envelope **average temperature in K** converted to C by subtracting exactly 273.15; source correction variant and record-count definition must be inspected in the real CSV before calculating any results. Do not use trait if the field cannot unambiguously be identified or provenance/units lack validation. Freeze minimum original source record count 20 and ≥100 sensitivity; no missing species as zero. If source publishes raw and corrected versions, **primary is published effort+climate/EVI corrected**, as warranted by source, and raw as sensitivity, not cherry-pick.

**Botanical samples:** Author-original exact first-party data from v314/v315, all source-file SHA256 checks in `source_matched_rail_road_v314.SOURCE_SHA256`, original 7 original railway flora programmes: Czech (39 whole stations), Italy (28 whole stations), Croatia-Bosnia (15 stations **one** paper), Slovenia (89 true railway sites), Slovakia (30 station-level marginal lists), Kashmir (16 authentic 2014 railway station Transects and same-station nearby Interior), and Western Australia (120 source-reported 2500-m² *railway-reserve vegetation*, not confirmed operational ballast). These are at most three **continents** (Europe/Asia/Oceania), not global geographic representation of railways. Add separately documented MIREN mountain ROAD references CZ, IT; city tree pits SI, HRBA; Malacky urban SK; Kashmir same-station Interior 1/2. **All source contrasts are different estimands**, not a unified railway vs unimpacted natural habitat pooled estimate. No site-level WA valid nonrail control in existing data.

## Exact estimands, primary and sensitivity
1. For every rail study `j`, calculate **global species temperature niche position** `G_j = sum(f_js * T_s)/sum(f_js)` over study taxa with exact species-level source names, EIDC global trait, no unresolved source names; `f_js` original site-level occupancy frequency (marginal for SK). Also equal-species CWM. **All original named source species are eligible**, never truncate to top-ranked taxa to improve sign.
2. Coverage = known original named taxa / source named taxa and original frequency mass in known / all valid named mass. MAIN coverage gates ≥15 species AND ≥50% occupancy-frequency mass in both arms, plus ≥50% named species coverage, *all fixed before outcomes*. Sensitivities low min20 vs high min100 EIDC underlying species observation count; presence- and frequency-weighted. When source coverage insufficient, display NA not hypothetical warmth.
3. For each **genuinely different documented reference** (CZ vs MIREN ROAD, IT vs MIREN ROAD, SI vs urban tree pits, HRBA vs urban tree pits, SK vs urban vegetation, Kashmir 2014 station railway vs same-station nearby Interior), calculate `Delta_j=G_rail - G_reference` on the EXACT same EIDC species trait map. Direction and sample coverage must be reported by reference type. Only Kashmir source has actual same-station matched ecological locality; other references are geographically or habitat mismatched and remain descriptive.
4. For Kashmir, recompute **true same-station paired mean Δ** (16 station pairs Track+Embankment vs both Interiors) for species matching global indicator, separate from regional pooled frequency difference, with >=8 stations passing >=3 species and both sides ≥50% coverage. Some local controls are ~150m near railway and might be affected, so not guaranteed pristine. Do not call 16 stations 16 independent climatic regions.
5. Remove provisional known C4 species group and `Cynodon dactylon` separately, frozen from v339 list. Classifications are NOT fully independently physiologically confirmed, so label tentative functional membership.
6. Benchmark only same independent 2023 European EIVE ordinal values where both original EIDC and EIVE known; descriptive country-specific rank correlation, not assumed independent studies. **Not** used to tune thresholds/selection.
7. Do not take a country-level pooled fixed effect or claim *temperature as causal selector* from unmatched source habitats. An all-study sign test is descriptive unless sample frame standardized; continents are effectively three. No global climate interaction with site temperature until comparable actual site temperatures and rail/nonrail pairs across independent regions are available.

## Separate mandatory access gate
Prior to treating Harris et al. 2022 as real input: query EIDC official data/CKAN metadata, download exact official file, verify CSV ZIP magic, source filename, byte size, SHA256, header/schema, row count, unique normalized botanical species, correct K temperature scale, identifiable record-count and effort/EVI correction columns. Reject guessed links returning HTML/403 or ambiguous fields. No effect calculated if EIDC source cannot be authenticated.

## Interpretational guardrails
- Global **plant climatic trait coverage** is not worldwide railway geographic coverage. In three continents, most actual rail records are European.
- Average GBIF occurrence climatology is realized envelope, **not fundamental tolerance, genetic adaptation, ballast surface microclimate or climate warming of a site**.
- Historical GBIF occurrence and modelling are geographically biased despite corrections; check influence of ≥100 records and regional/native range.
- WA is *railway reserve*, may encompass background native habitat; not guaranteed train-influenced plot. Exclude from claims of active ballast.
- Original source authors' raw species×location or survey data are NOT committed; only aggregated metrics, stable source hashes and workflow reproducibility.

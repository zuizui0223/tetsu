## Key literature and research gap
- *Railways as a source of alien plants* (2024), https://preslia.cz/article/11570 : 39 Czech railway localities; 764 taxa; track assemblages and surrounding habitats differed.
- Li et al. (2026), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301479726018402 : China–Laos railway station disturbance linked to alien plant richness; does not isolate transport.
- Benedetti & Morelli (2017), https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0183691 : alien plant hotspots align with roads/rail; matching both networks is essential.
- Systematic review (2024), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11376103/ : corridor function for vascular plants remains insufficiently tested.
- Oilseed rape viable-seed spillage at rail/pipeline transportation (2017), https://doi.org/10.3389/fevo.2017.00104 : rail switchyards, grain cargo and ports motivate explicit freight tests.
- Cargo traffic and railway-area alien richness (2025), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0367253025001094 : local traffic associations exist; topology beyond roads could be distinctive.
